Sweet Dahlia — Bremerton

Lunch is here in Bremerton!

Join us Thursday & Friday, 10 am–2 pm for fresh, scratch-made lunches at the Sweet Dahlia kitchen. Handcrafted sandwiches on our house-made rolls, pressed on the grill:

  • Roasted Turkey Pesto
  • Cubano
  • Roasted Veggie
  • BLT
  • Grilled Cheese

You'll also find house-made quiche, fresh salads, scratch-baked cookies, our famous donuts, and plenty of sweet treats.

Dinner Club pickup now available in Bremerton on Saturdays, 10 am–2 pm.
