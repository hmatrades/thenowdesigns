What's Dinner Club?

Order our easy, delicious take-home dinners — a new meal every week. Fresh, fully prepared, local, and ready to heat in your oven in under 10 minutes. No obligation, just good food.

Order OnlineSign Up for the Weekly Text

How it works

  1. 1

    The Menu

    Members get the weekly menu by text Wednesday morning. Each menu offers a couple of choices, including wheat-free and vegetarian options. Dinners range from nostalgic home cooking (meatloaf, pot pies, mac & cheese) to international dishes (tamales, osso buco, lasagna) and gourmet meals like Beef Bourguignon and Duck Confit.

  2. 2

    The Order

    Order online anytime from when you get the menu until Saturday 6 pm. Choose your items, your pickup day, and your pickup time.

  3. 3

    The Pickup

    Come by during your selected window. Need to change something? Just text us.

Pickup locations

  • Bainbridge Island (Coppertop) — Thursday, Friday & Saturday
  • Bremerton — Saturdays

Our dinners are packaged cold with simple reheating instructions — usually 8–20 minutes in the oven. Many freeze beautifully, too. Prefer it served warm and ready to eat? Just leave a note at checkout. You don't have to be a member to order, but items can sell out, so let us know early.
