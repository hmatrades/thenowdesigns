Day-tripping from Seattle?

Fresh off the Bainbridge ferry

Sweet Dahlia is the locals' answer to "best donuts on Bainbridge Island" — a quick ten-minute drive up from the Winslow ferry dock. Grab a giant apple fritter and a Costa Rican pour-over for the ride to Bloedel Reserve or the beach at Fay Bainbridge, then take a box of hand-dipped donuts back on the boat. Your friends will ask where you found them.
