Our Story

It's What Community Tastes Like.

Whether you're starting your morning with a handcrafted Costa Rican pour-over coffee, grabbing lunch on our scratch-made bread, picking up an after-school treat, taking home Dinner Club, or packing up for your next Pacific Northwest adventure, we're honored to be a small part of your story. From everyday moments to life's biggest celebrations, we're here to bring people together with food made with care.

Meet the team

Chris is our executive chef and runs our bakery. He spent years as pastry chef at a Lake Tahoe ski resort and nearly a decade as sous chef for a top San Francisco Bay Area catering company, where he worked alongside chefs like Julia Child, Alice Waters, and Jeremiah Tower and cooked for a long list of notable clients.

Stacey runs the business side of Sweet Dahlia. A certified counselor and board-certified in biofeedback, she puts those skills to work growing the bakery while raising the family. When her daughter went dairy-, wheat-, and soy-free, the family dove into gluten-free alternative cooking — and never looked back, which is why so many of our items have wheat-free options.

We're proud to call Kitsap County home, to source ingredients as locally as possible, and to be a member of the Bainbridge Island Chamber of Commerce. Thanks for trying our Sweet Dahlia creations — stop in and say hello.

— Stacey Wadkins
